=== M-REP-C review round 1 — operation-result unwrap/panic sweep (2026-10-16) ===

Scope.  The verified/phantom module: amari-core/src/verified.rs and
amari-core/src/verified_contracts.rs.  Round-scoped exhaustive sweep of every
unwrap / expect / panic! / unreachable! / todo! / unimplemented! path reachable
from finite constructed inputs, plus a method-by-method walk of the operation
surface named by the dispatch (add, sub, neg, geometric_product, compose,
apply, inverse).  No full corpus archive; this file is the durable record
(method + counts + items).

Method
  1. Grep the two sources:
       grep -nE 'unwrap|expect|panic!|unreachable!|todo!|unimplemented!' \
         amari-core/src/verified.rs amari-core/src/verified_contracts.rs
  2. Classify every hit:
       (R) runtime path reachable from finite constructed inputs;
       (A) specification-annotation text — compiled away by the dummy Creusot
           proc-macro under the pinned `formal-verification` feature recipe
           (see "Pinned annotation semantics" below); a proof obligation under
           real Creusot, never a runtime branch;
       (T) test-only.
  3. For each operation on the named surface, decide "provably total on finite
     inputs" (returns without panicking) or "made fallible with the contract
     updated".  A fallible operation is routed through
     VerifiedMultivector::new, whose finiteness + length gate is the single
     checked boundary.

Pinned annotation semantics (why (A) is not a panic path)
  `#[requires]`/`#[ensures]` come from creusot-contracts 0.8.  Under a normal
  `cargo build`/`cargo test` (which is what the pinned recipe runs, with
  `--features formal-verification` but without the `creusot` cfg), the proc
  macro is the *dummy* implementation: creusot-contracts-proc-0.8.0/src/dummy.rs
  returns the function item unchanged and discards the attribute expression.
  The body is therefore never type-checked or evaluated at runtime.  Proof
  discharge is a separate pre-1.0 gate (matrix C3/C4) and no proof tool is
  invoked by this package.

Residuals
  - Real-Creusot proof discharge is not attempted; the annotations are
    EXPRESSED, not discharged (matrix C3/C4).
  - VerifiedMultivector::add / geometric_product / apply_to_multivector remain
    infallible arithmetic: they are panic-free, but their finite inputs can
    overflow to ±inf.  Callers who need the type's finite-coefficient invariant
    use the checked wrappers (verified_addition, verified_scalar_mult,
    verify_scalar_identity/zero_addition) or VerifiedMultivector::new.
